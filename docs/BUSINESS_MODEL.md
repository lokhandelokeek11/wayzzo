# Business Model — Wayzo

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Delivery Commission (Primary)
- Platform takes **20% commission** on every delivery fare paid
- Partner receives 80% of fare
- Applies to all order types (B2C and B2B)

### 2. Subscription Plans (Merchants)
| Plan | Price/Month | Included Deliveries | Per-Extra Delivery |
|------|-------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Starter | ₹999 | 100 | ₹12 |
| Growth | ₹2,999 | 400 | ₹10 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Negotiated |

### 3. API Access (Enterprise)
- Metered API pricing for high-volume integrations
- SLA-backed uptime guarantees
- Dedicated partner pool option

### 4. Surge Revenue Share
- During peak hours, 10% of surge premium retained by platform (rest passed to partner)

### 5. Partner Incentive Programs (Cost Centre → LTV Builder)
- Weekly performance bonuses funded by platform margin
- Drives partner retention and quality

## Unit Economics (Target — City at Scale)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Average Order Value (AOV) | ₹120 |
| Platform Revenue per Order | ₹24 (20% commission) |
| Variable Cost per Order | ₹8 (payments, infra, SMS) |
| Contribution Margin | ₹16 per order |
| Target Daily Orders per City | 2,000 |
| Daily Revenue (1 city) | ₹48,000 |

## Go-To-Market Strategy

1. **City Launch**: Begin with Bengaluru, targeting local merchants (restaurants, pharmacies, boutiques)
2. **Supply First**: Recruit and verify 500 delivery partners before demand launch
3. **Merchant Acquisition**: Direct sales to 100 anchor merchants
4. **Consumer Acquisition**: Referral program, social media, local influencers
5. **Expansion**: Replicate city playbook to Mumbai, Delhi NCR (Month 4+)
